# November 13, 2026: Hackpad Complete Design and Fabrication

- **Electronics:** Designed the custom printed circuit board (PCB) using KiCad, placed components, and routed traces for the Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller, mechanical switches, rotary encoder, and OLED display. Generated production files (Gerber, CPL, BOM).
- **3D Enclosure:** Modeled a custom plastic case for the hackpad in Fusion 360 to house the electronics securely, and exported design and source files.
- **Bill of Materials:** Created and verified the BOM.csv file listing all required parts, prices, and purchase links.
